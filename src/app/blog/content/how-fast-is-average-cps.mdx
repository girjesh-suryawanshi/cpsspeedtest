## How Fast is the Average CPS? (And How Do You Compare?)

You've clicked your heart out on a [CPS test](/cps-test), and a number pops up on the screen. But what does that number actually mean? Are you a clicking god, or are you clicking in slow motion?

Understanding the average Clicks Per Second (CPS) is the first step to knowing where you stand and what you should be aiming for. Let's break down the numbers and see what "fast" really means.

### The Global Average: What Are Most People Clicking?

For the average person just browsing the internet or working on a document, the click speed is pretty relaxed. They aren't trying to win a 1v1 fight; they're just opening folders.

-   **Average Human CPS: 3-6 Clicks Per Second**

Most non-gamers fall into this range. It’s a comfortable, sustainable pace for everyday computer use. If you ask your mom or dad to take a CPS test, they'll likely land somewhere in this zone.

### The Gamer Average: What's "Normal" in Gaming?

This is where things start to speed up. When you're gaming, especially in fast-paced titles, a higher CPS becomes a necessity.

-   **Average Gamer CPS: 6-9 Clicks Per Second**

This is the sweet spot for a majority of the gaming community. A player hitting 7 or 8 CPS is considered to have a decent click speed. They can keep up in most online games and perform most actions without feeling held back by their clicking speed. If you're in this range, you're doing great!

### What is Considered a "Fast" CPS?

Alright, now we're moving from "average" to "impressive." When people talk about having a "fast" click speed, they are usually talking about hitting double digits.

-   **Fast CPS: 10-14 Clicks Per Second**

Hitting 10 CPS or more consistently puts you in the top tier of clickers. This is the range where you start to see a real, tangible advantage in PvP games. Players at this level are often using clicking techniques beyond just normal clicking, like a bit of jitter or a relaxed butterfly style.

### The Elite Tier: The 1% of Clickers

This is the territory of the truly dedicated, the players you see on YouTube, the ones who make clicking an art form.

-   **Elite CPS: 15+ Clicks Per Second**

Anyone consistently clicking above 15 CPS is at a pro level. This speed is almost always achieved through mastering advanced techniques like **[Butterfly Clicking](/butterfly-click-test)** or **[Drag Clicking](/drag-click-test)**. It's not necessary for most games, but it provides a massive advantage in reaction-based combat.

### Does Age Affect Average CPS?

Yes, it does! Generally, reaction times and motor skills are at their peak in the late teens and early 20s.

-   **Ages 14-25:** This group typically has the highest average CPS. Their reflexes are sharp, and they've often grown up playing video games.
-   **Ages 25-40:** The average tends to dip slightly but remains quite high, especially for those who continue to game regularly.
-   **Ages 40+:** The average CPS tends to be lower, as reaction times naturally slow over time. However, a dedicated older gamer can still be incredibly fast!

### The Minecraft Factor

In the Minecraft community, the perception of "average" is often a bit higher because the game benefits so much from a fast click speed.

-   **Casual Minecraft Player:** 6-8 CPS
-   **Serious PvP Player:** 9-13 CPS
-   **Hardcore Hypixel Player:** 12-16+ CPS

### Conclusion

Don't be discouraged if your score isn't in the "Elite" tier right away. Your CPS is just a number, and it's a number you can improve with practice. Use these averages as a benchmark, not as a strict rule. The most important thing is to find a speed that works for you and helps you enjoy your games more.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**1. Is it possible to have a 0 CPS?**
Only if you don't click at all! Even a single click in a 5-second test will give you a 0.2 CPS.

**2. What's more important: a high CPS or good aim?**
Good aim is almost always more important. A player with 8 CPS and perfect aim will beat a player with 15 CPS who can't hit their target. The ideal is to have both!

**3. Do laptops' trackpads have a lower average CPS?**
Yes, significantly. It's much harder to click quickly on a trackpad than on a proper mouse. The averages discussed here are all based on using a computer mouse.

**4. How can I find my average CPS?**
The best way is to take a few tests and average the results. Don't just rely on one test. Take our [5-second CPS test](/cps-test) three times in a row and average your scores for a reliable number.

**5. Is it bad to have a below-average CPS?**
Not at all! For 99% of games and all regular computer use, an "average" or "below-average" CPS is perfectly fine. The obsession with high CPS is really only a major factor in a few specific gaming communities.
