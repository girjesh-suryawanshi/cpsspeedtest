# How Fast is the Average CPS? (And How Do You Compare?)

We all want to be fast. In the world of gaming, speed can be the difference between victory and defeat. One of the most common benchmarks for a player's raw mechanical skill is their Clicks Per Second, or CPS.

But it's easy to see pro players hitting insane numbers and feel slow in comparison. So, what is a "normal" CPS? What's the average? And what's considered genuinely fast?

Let's break it down so you can see exactly where you stand. The first step is to get your own score. If you haven't already, take a few seconds and try our **[CPS Speed Test](/cps-test)**. Go ahead, I'll wait.

Got your score? Great. Now let's see what it means.

## The Global Average: What Can Most People Do?

For the average person who doesn't play video games competitively, the typical CPS score falls somewhere between **3 and 6 clicks per second**.

This is the speed of someone browsing the internet, working on a spreadsheet, or casually playing a game. It's perfectly functional for everyday tasks. If you're in this range, you're perfectly normal!

## The Gamer Average: Stepping It Up

This is where things start to get more competitive. For someone who plays games regularly, especially PC games, the average CPS is higher.

The average CPS for a gamer is typically between **7 and 9 clicks per second**.

Players in this range have better mouse control and finger dexterity from hours of gaming. This speed is more than enough to be competitive in most online games. If you're hitting 8 CPS, you're already faster than a large portion of the player base.

## The Minecraft PvP Average

Minecraft is a unique case because high CPS is so vital for PvP. The averages here are naturally higher.

For a dedicated Minecraft PvP player, the average CPS is often between **8 and 12 clicks per second**.

Players in this range are likely using techniques like Jitter or Butterfly clicking to get an edge. Hitting a consistent 10-12 CPS in a fight is a significant advantage. If you're a Minecraft player, you might want to try our special **[Minecraft CPS Test](/minecraft-cps-test)** to see how you stack up in a more familiar environment.

## What is Considered "Fast" CPS?

So, when do you cross the line from "average" to "fast"? Here’s a general breakdown:

| CPS Score | Rank      | Description                                     |
|-----------|-----------|-------------------------------------------------|
| 3-6       | Casual    | Standard speed for non-gamers.                  |
| 7-9       | Fast      | A solid speed for most competitive gamers.      |
| 10-13     | Very Fast | Elite speed, requires practice and technique.   |
| 14+       | Pro-Level | World-class speed, usually using advanced methods. |

If you're hitting **10 CPS or more** with regular clicking, you have legitimately fast fingers. To get beyond that, most players need to learn advanced techniques.

## Factors That Influence Your CPS

Your score isn't just about your fingers. Several things can affect it:

1.  **Your Mouse**: A good gaming mouse with low-latency switches makes a huge difference. A cheap office mouse will hold you back.
2.  **Your Technique**: As we've seen, techniques like Butterfly and Drag clicking are designed specifically to maximize CPS.
3.  **Your Age**: Younger players often have faster reaction times and can achieve higher CPS. However, with practice, anyone can improve.
4.  **Practice**: Nobody is born with a 15 CPS. It's a skill that is developed over hundreds of hours of practice.

## How Do You Compare to the Pros?

Professional esports players and top-tier Minecraft players often have a CPS of **12-16** during intense moments. Streamers who specialize in techniques like drag clicking for bridging can hit short bursts of **30-60 CPS**, but this is not used for aiming or combat.

Don't compare your score to a drag clicker's burst speed. A more realistic comparison is a pro player's sustained combat CPS, which is typically in that 12-16 range.

### FAQ Section

**1. Is it possible to have a 20 CPS with regular clicking?**
It's extremely rare. Almost all scores above 15 CPS are achieved using Jitter, Butterfly, or Drag clicking. A 20 CPS with a single finger would be a world record.

**2. Does a higher screen refresh rate (Hz) help with CPS?**
Not directly for your clicking speed, but it can make the game feel smoother and more responsive, which might help your overall performance, including how quickly you react and click.

**3. I get a different score on other websites. Why?**
Different websites can have slightly different ways of measuring clicks or handling browser lag. For the most consistent results, it's best to stick to one testing site to track your progress.

**4. Is it better to have a high CPS or good aim?**
Both are important, but most pro players would agree that good aim is more valuable than an extremely high CPS. It's better to land 80% of your 8 CPS shots than 20% of your 14 CPS shots.

**5. How much can I realistically improve my CPS?**
With consistent practice, most players can improve their CPS by 2-3 points within a few weeks. If you learn a new technique like butterfly clicking, you could potentially double your score over time.
