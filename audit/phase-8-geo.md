3. **Debounce Delay & Switch Physics**:
   - Technical breakdown of microswitch leaf bounce vs optical beam interruption, explaining the physical mechanics of why mice like the Glorious Model O or Roccat Kone Pro double-click at 2ms–4ms debounce thresholds.

## 2. Rejection of GEO Myths & Manipulative Tactics

To ensure long-term trust and strict compliance with Google's Search Spam Policies:
- **No Invisible Prompt Injections**: Zero hidden text, white-on-white text, or system prompt instructions designed to fool crawlers.
- **No Fabricated Author Credentials**: Eliminated fictional personas; all content is attributed to the authentic CpsSpeedTest Editorial Team.
- **No Fake Ratings**: Eliminated fraudulent `AggregateRating` structured data.
- **No Artificial Entity Stuffing**: Avoided keyword lists or repetitive synthetic text.
- **Transparent Boundaries**: Disclosed that the tool measures physical browser input cadence, not medical or neurological reflexes.
