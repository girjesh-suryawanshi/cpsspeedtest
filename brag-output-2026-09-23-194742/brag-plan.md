# Brag Plan: CpsSpeedTest

## What is this app?
A browser-based gaming hub featuring 30+ free skill-based games to test and improve clicking speed, reaction time, memory, and typing skills.

## The angle
A fast-paced, high-energy arcade speed test challenge. The video leans into the intense, competitive nature of clicking and reaction speed, using the site's neon-on-dark aesthetic to feel like an esports training tool.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The question "How fast can you click?" slamming onto the screen word-by-word, immediately followed by the animated CPS Gauge filling up rapidly.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The CPS Gauge maxing out at 14.2 CPS.
- A rapid sequence of game cards sliding in (Time Guesser, Speed Typer, Aim Trainer) to show the variety.
- The "Cheetah" rank badge popping up to show the competitive tiering.

## Outro / punchline
"Global Leaderboard." The final call to action to compete worldwide.

## User flow worth showing
Land on homepage -> gauge spikes to 14.2 CPS -> game grid reveals -> final rank badge.

## Tone
- Preset: chaotic
- Creative direction: High-energy arcade speedrun
- Interpretation: Fast cuts, heavy use of hard beat-driven transitions, text that slams in and holds, high-BPM music to match the clicking intensity.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 18 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: hsl(222.2, 84%, 4.9%)
- Accent: hsl(172.2, 91.2%, 59.8%)
- Text: hsl(210, 40%, 98%)
- Display font: Inter
- Body font: Inter
- Strongest visual element: The animated CPS gauge.

## Share copy (draft)
Think your reflexes are fast? Test your clicking speed, reaction time, and memory. Play 30+ free skill games at CpsSpeedTest.

## Audio direction
- Role: dense rhythmic layer
- Music: upbeat chaotic arcade track
- Music treatment: hard start, high volume throughout, abrupt cut on the final logo.
- Music cue guidance: detect at composition via analyze_music_cues.py / hyperframes beats. Use strong cues for scene transitions; use beat grid for sequential reveals.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; use music RMS/bass to make the hero gauge glow and pulse.
- SFX posture: dense; motion-matched; fast clicking and snapping.
- Audio-coupled moments: gauge filling coupled with a rising pitch or rapid clicking SFX, game cards revealing on beat grid.
- Restraint rule: Avoid generic corporate background music. Must sound intense.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The Challenge — 3.0s
Text slams in word-by-word: "How" "fast" "can" "you" "CLICK?"
Sequential/interaction: Text appears word-by-word.
Audio intent: Intense intro hit, fast rhythmic clicking sound.
Audio-coupled idea: beat-aligned reveal for each word.
Music: chaotic
Transition mood: hard → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The Gauge — 5.0s
The CPS Gauge from the homepage. The needle rapidly swings from left to right, numbers tick up wildly until settling at 14.2 CPS.
Sequential/interaction: Simulated rapid clicking driving the gauge up.
Audio intent: Rising tension, intense ticking building to a climax.
Audio-coupled idea: Counter ticks aligned to beats.
Transition mood: flash → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — The Games — 5.0s
Grid of 3 game cards slides in from the bottom one by one (CPS Test, Aim Trainer, Speed Typer).
Sequential/interaction: Cards appear one by one.
Audio intent: Heavy synth hits for each card.
Audio-coupled idea: Sequential reveals on the beat grid.
Transition mood: hard slide → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — The Rank / Outro — 5.0s
The "Cheetah (7-10 CPS)" rank card pops in. Then the text: "Compete Globally."
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: Final heavy payoff hit.
Audio-coupled idea: none
Transition mood: hard cut to black

**Music mood for this video:** chaotic
**Audio summary:** Fast, heavy beats that mirror the intensity of clicking 14 times a second, with sharp syncopated hits for visual reveals.
